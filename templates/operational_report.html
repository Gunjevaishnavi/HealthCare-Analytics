Formal Boundaries & Limitations Statement

Scope & Acceptance Criteria Notice: This analytics system is strictly engineered for educational and computer science research purposes. The records processed are synthetic, mathematically simulated cohorts designed to demonstrate data engineering, SQL relational mapping, REST API architectures, and transparent statistical rule engines.

  • No Clinical Diagnostic Claims: The calculated indicators (e.g., R-VIT-01, R-COM-05) do NOT represent clinical diagnoses, prognostic criteria, or treatment plans.
  • No Bedside Decision Support: This system must NEVER be used to triage live patients, recommend medications, or alter patient care.
  • Synthetic Data Boundaries: All identifiers, physiological values, and demographic fields are entirely synthetic or de-identified.
